# Visual quality and mobile target

Confirmed by the user: keep GolfWorld a browser/PWA for iPhone, iPad, Android
phones/tablets, and desktop. Keep the existing React/R3F/Three renderer.
The current gameplay slice stays playable throughout art improvements.

First upgrade: a smooth, textured, rigged starter character with clothing
detail, blended walking/idle motion, and local shadows. Keep current outfit
color customization and a rotatable 3D phone preview. The shipped model is
stylized; a finished realistic human needs a dedicated art asset. Ship each
asset with its source and usage record. Keep the walking/next-shot cameras
close enough to see the avatar; retain the stable wider ball-flight view.

Later art work: dedicated golf swing animations with club/hand alignment,
editable face/hair/body variations, better skin/eye materials, authored lots
and clubhouse meshes, terrain/vegetation textures, coherent daylight and
reflections. Profile a representative phone and tablet before increasing
shadow, texture, animation or visible-player costs. Scale resolution and
detail to sustained performance, not screen size alone.

GTA 6 is a visual reference, not a promise of identical technology or output.
Achieving comparable fidelity throughout a city requires professional art,
animation, lighting and extensive optimization. A native engine or remote
GPU streaming would change this project's architecture; per the collaboration
rule, propose either in a separate user/Grok review before implementing it.
Neither is part of this avatar pass. Text/photo likeness generation remains
in the existing city/social/avatar design review.
